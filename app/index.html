    Sources & honesty notes

    • Every trophy's requirement comes from the official PlayStation trophy list as published (Exophase & PSNProfiles listed it on 18 Nov 2026 and agree on all 55 names, descriptions and grades). Each guide below names that source beside it.

    • Missable flags are read from the official descriptions, not from playing the game: anything tied to one mission, chapter or choice is flagged. The game launched 19 Nov 2026; if mission replay turns out to cover a flagged trophy, the flag still did its job — it made you do it first.
